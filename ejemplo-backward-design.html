Lesson Plan Template — Backward Design / Diseño Inverso

Universidad de Córdoba · Bilingüismo para la Paz · Autoras: Ana María Arévalo Patiño (Mocarí) & Rosa Ballesteros (La Ribera)

Stage 1: Desired Results / Resultados deseados

  • Topic / Tema: International Cultures & The Beauty of Colombia (Unit 4)
  • Guiding / Inquiry Question: How can we describe and compare Colombian landmarks with other cultures in English?
  • Measurable Learning Outcomes: By the end of the 6-hour Saturday session, students (ages 8–13) will produce at least 4 spoken sentences using comparatives and superlatives.

Stage 2: Acceptable Evidence / Evidencia aceptable

  • Formative Assessment: Oral participation checklist during Giant Dice & Corner Sprints relays.
  • Performance Task: Paired roleplay cards comparing two cultural landmarks.
  • Summative Review: Mega-Review game (Trashketball) prior to the written assessment.

Stage 3: Learning Plan / Plan de aprendizaje

  • Different Considerations: Visual flashcards with cartoon illustrations and large fonts to support diverse reading paces.
  • Warm-Up (30 min): Active TPR icebreaker & vocabulary activation.
  • Core Activities (3.5 h): Station rotations — Fly Swatter, Scavenger Relays, and Giant Dice.
  • Production & Closure (2 h): Guided roleplay presentations, feedback, and reflection.
